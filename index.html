Personal Overview

IELTS 7.5 HK

Dr. Sizhe CHEN (陈思哲) is a Ph.D. candidate in Medical Sciences at The Chinese University of Hong Kong (CUHK), supervised by Prof. Qi Su. He holds a Master of Science in Genomics & Bioinformatics from CUHK and a Bachelor of Engineering in Bioengineering from Dalian University of Technology (985 / Double First-Class).

His research focuses on antimicrobial peptides (AMPs), evolutionary biology of the gut microbiome, and AI-aided drug discovery. He has published more than 11 SCI papers, with 7 as co-first author in journals including Nature Communications, Microbiome, Cell Reports, and Journal of Agricultural and Food Chemistry.

He was awarded the CUHK Talent Development Scholarship (2026, 全校 5 人/年), the “Model DUT · Youth Figure · Science and Technology Innovation Award” (2023, 全校 3 人/年), and the iGEM Gold Medal (2021) with two Best Project nominations as team captain.
