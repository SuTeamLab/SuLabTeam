Personal Overview

    Dr.Sizhe CHEN is an investigator in molecular biology, bioinformatics and AI-aided drugs discovery, with extensive experience in the antimicrobial peptides (AMPs) and their relationship to evolutionary biology.

    He received his received his Bachelor's degree in Bioengineering from Dalian University of Technology (2019–2023), a 985/211 and Double First-Class university in China. His undergraduate thesis was supervised by Professor Tian Liu and his thesis was awarded the university's Outstanding Undergraduate Thesis Award (2 recipients per year per major). He also received the "Model DUT·Youth Figure·Science and Technology Innovation Award" (3 recipients per year university-wide).

    He received his Master's Degree in Science from Chinese University of Hong Kong (Genomics & Bioinformatics, supervisor: Professor Stephen Tsui).

    He is currently a PhD candidate at Chinese University of Hong Kong (Medical Sciences, supervisor: Professor Su Qi).

    He is committed to the the antimicrobial peptides (AMPs) with its relationship to evolutionary biology and AI-aided drugs discovery. He has published more than 11 SCI papers, 7 of which were published as the co-first author in the Nature Communications (IF: 18.1, JCR Q1), Microbime (IF: 14.9, JCR Q1), Cell Reports (IF: 7.9, JCR Q1), Journal of Agricultural and Food Chemistry (IF: 6.6, JCR Q1) and other internationally renowned journals.
